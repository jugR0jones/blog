# Financial Cost
The immediate financial cost is immense. Especially in the country I live it.

A short while ago I purchased an AMD AI 9 HX470 with 64Gb of ram for approximately $1 720,54. It was discounted.

A number of factors influenced the price:
* 64Gb ram. The ram is soldered which made it cheaper, but a 32Gb stick on its own is almost half the cost of the unit. Unless miracles happened I would never be able to afford the upgrade.
* Occulink. I wanted the port so that I could purchase an external GPU at some stage. This seems like a far more likely purchase than the ram, but still too expensive.
* AMD APU. I could have picked up a model with an Intel processor that was faster but I'm not convinced Intel is the appropriate choice at the moment. Apple's products perform better but they are almost twice the price.
* Income. The money that was used for this purchase should have gone elsewhere in my life. It wasn't disposable income. Not the best move but I'm hoping I can make it work. This reduces the risk which reduces the costs.

# Time
I've owned the device for around a month before I started measuring the cost.

I can use smaller models and "burn" through tokens but quite often the result is not the result I expected. Larger models are slower but often produce better results.

Trying to get a large model to run on this hardware takes time.
